# Intake 04 — News, Press, Awards, Books

**All four of these sections are optional.**

Write `DELETE THIS SECTION` under any heading and I'll remove the page and its
navigation tab entirely.

That is genuinely the right call when you have little to put there. A News page
with two entries reads as an abandoned site. No News page reads as a deliberate
one. You can add these back at any point.

Hand this back and I'll write it into `website/content/news.json`.

---

## E1 · News highlights

**Needs an image per entry.** Usually the project it's about, or a photograph of
the award or the magazine spread.

These read as **sentences, not headlines**. The bold runs are what create the
visual hierarchy — mark them with `**double asterisks**` and I'll convert them.

Good:
> `**Riverside House** wins **Best Residential Interior** at the **2026 Trends Awards**.`

> `**Courtyard House** featured as the **Cover Story** in **Architectural Digest India**, March 2026.`

Not:
> `Award Win!` — that's a headline, and it says nothing.

## A note on all four

These pages exist on the reference site because that practice has thirty years
of accumulated recognition. If you're earlier than that, the honest move is to
delete what's empty and let the work speak.

The one exception: **if you have even two or three awards, keep the Awards page.**
It's the section prospective clients look for, and unlike News it doesn't look
stale when it's short — an award from 2024 is still an award.

- What you do — architecture, interiors, landscape, furniture?
- The kind of work and the kind of client
- Anything notable — awards, publications, well-known projects
- Where you're based and where you work

## C2 · Design Philosophy

One or two paragraphs. This is where the practice's actual thinking goes.

How do you approach a new site? A brief? What do you believe about materials,
about climate, about how people use buildings? What would you refuse to do?

Specific beats abstract. "We start every project by asking what the site already
wants to be" says more than "we create thoughtful, contextual architecture".

**Headshots:** square-ish framing, and — this matters more than quality — make
them **consistent**. Same background, same distance, same rough lighting.
Mismatched snapshots are very obvious once they're lined up in a grid.

They display in greyscale and turn colour when someone hovers over them, which
hides a lot of minor inconsistency in colour but none in framing.

**Roles:** keep them short and parallel. `Senior Architect`, `Architect`,
`Interior Designer`, `Studio Manager` — not `Senior Architect & Head of
Sustainability Research`.

Four fill-in forms. Open them in any text editor, type your answers under each
heading, hand them back to me, and I'll convert them into the site.

## Order of work

Do these in sequence. Each one makes the site usable on its own, so you can stop
at any point and still have something coherent.

1. **`01-studio-details.md`** — quickest win. Removes every fake phone number and
   email address from the site.
2. **`02-about.md`** — the highest-value writing you will do. A prospective
   client reads this before they look at a single project.
3. **`03-projects.md`, one project only** — do one completely, text and photos.
   It proves the pipeline works end to end and gives you a template.
4. **The remaining projects.**
5. **Home page** (section B, at the end of form 01) — chosen from the images you
   now actually have.
6. **`04-news-press-awards.md`** — only if there's something real to say.

---

## The launch minimum

You do **not** need to fill everything. The site has 125 image slots because
that's what a thirty-year-old practice fills. To go live you need:

- 4–6 projects, 6–10 photos each
- 3–5 home slideshow images
- 1 portrait per principal
- 2–3 paragraphs on the firm
- 2–4 paragraphs per project
- Address, phone, emails

Roughly 45–70 images and 1,200 words.

**Six good projects beat twelve thin ones.** An architecture practice is judged
on the quality of what it shows.

---

## Photos

Give me the **originals**. Full size, straight from the photographer. Do not
resize, crop or compress anything first — the pipeline does all of that, and
every manual edit loses quality it can't get back.

10–15 MB files are ideal. 50 MB is fine. 300 DPI print files are fine. CMYK is
fine. Sideways phone photos are fine.

Minimum useful width: **2400px** for heroes, **2000px** for gallery images.
Below that they get upscaled and look soft — that's the one thing I can't fix.

Drop them into the matching folder in `../image-prep/inbox/` (there's a
`_README.txt` in each explaining exactly what it produces), or just tell me where
they are.

## Filling these in

- Type under the dashed lines, replacing `TBD`.
- Anything left as `TBD` stays a placeholder, and I'll list it for you.
- `DELETE THIS SECTION` removes that section from the site entirely.
- Don't worry about formatting, punctuation style, or length precision. Write it
  however it comes out — I'll tidy it.

---

## If you'd rather just talk it through

You don't have to fill in forms. Tell me about a project the way you'd describe
it to someone, and I'll draft the copy and hand it back for you to correct. Most
people find editing a draft much easier than facing a blank page.

One practical note: this conversation has a length limit. If we're writing all
six project descriptions together, do them a couple at a time and ask me to save
to the files after each batch. The files persist; the conversation doesn't.

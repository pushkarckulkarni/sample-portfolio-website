FONTS GO HERE
============================================================
Download Jost and Inter from Google Fonts, subset them to
Latin, and save them with exactly these names:

    jost-300.woff2
    jost-400.woff2
    jost-500.woff2
    inter-300.woff2
    inter-400.woff2

The @font-face rules are already written in
assets/css/base.css — nothing else to do.

Until these files exist the site falls back to
Futura / Century Gothic / system-ui, which looks close
but not identical.

Full instructions: docs/08-DEPLOY.md

IMAGE INBOX
============================================================
Put your original photographs here — any size, any resolution,
straight from the camera or the photographer. 15 MB files are fine.

WHERE THINGS GO
    home/hero/               homepage banner slideshow
    home/hero-mobile/        the same slides, portrait crop for phones
    projects/<slug>/         cover.jpg, hero.jpg and gallery/
    about/principals/        principal portraits
    about/team/              team headshots
    news/                    news card images

Each folder has a _README.txt telling you exactly what it produces.

THEN RUN ONE OF THESE
    python3 image-prep/studio.py      visual control panel (recommended)
    python3 image-prep/process.py     straight from the terminal

AND THEN
    python3 tools/build.py            rebuild the site

Originals are moved to image-prep/archive/ after processing,
so nothing is ever destroyed.
